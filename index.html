How Shared Links Work

By default, browsers keep local edits private. To make your links visible to anyone who opens the link, publish your updates to the repository's links.json file, or use an instant Snapshot Share Link!

Method 1: Instant Snapshot Share Link (No Setup Required)

Generates a special link encoding your exact websites and categories. Anyone who clicks this link will see your exact board immediately.

Method 2: Publish Directly to GitHub (Permanent for All Visitors)

Pushes your updated links directly to imtiahmed001-web/Web-Link-Manager so that everyone opening https://imtiahmed001-web.github.io/Web-Link-Manager/ gets your updates automatically.

Requires 'Contents: Read and write' access to imtiahmed001-web/Web-Link-Manager.
